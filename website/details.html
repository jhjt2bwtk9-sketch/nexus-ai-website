Requirements & Operations

Operating an AI automation agency for businesses and smart apartments requires a mix of cloud software, specialized API services, dedicated hosting, and physical IoT hardware.

On the software side, you do not need to build your own AI models. You rely on cloud infrastructure like OpenAI or Anthropic and workflow automation platforms like Make.com. To keep client data private, you deploy n8n or Home Assistant using affordable VPS services like Hetzner.

For smart apartments, use Home Assistant. You will need hardware like a Zigbee gateway, relays, and thermostats.

Operationally, you need a valid IT consulting trade license. Smart home installations require partnering with a licensed electrician for high-voltage wiring. You should also invest in business liability insurance and a GDPR-compliant privacy policy.
